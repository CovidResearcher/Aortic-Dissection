Disclaimer – No Claim of Routine Spontaneity in Human Aortic Dissection
This repository does not claim that hydrodynamic cavitation, micro-jet impact, balloon-expansion, or jet-hammer mechanisms occur routinely or spontaneously as primary drivers of human aortic dissection.  
Established pathophysiology (hypertension, medial degeneration, genetic aortopathies, bicuspid aortic valve, and related factors) remains primary. Any modeled contribution from the described micro-mechanical events is presented only as a specialized, theoretical possibility that would require prior wall weakening.  
The progressive degradation process that can lower remaining tensile strength is formalized in:
https://github.com/CovidResearcher/Aortic-Dissection/blob/main/degradation_failure.py  
That script implements the limit relation
\( \mathrm{UTS_{degraded}}(t) = \mathrm{UTS}_0 \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{matrix}}(t) \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{fatigue}}(t) \cdot \Phi_{\mathrm{geometry}}(t) \)
and illustrates cumulative biochemical and cyclic-fatigue effects over time. Parameters are illustrative. The model does not constitute evidence of routine spontaneity.
