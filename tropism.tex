In the repository’s own wording (README), the phrase is “gut-heart-lung axes” and the Biochemical Priming Phase is explicitly described as “rooted in tissue tropism.”
Tropism here means biological tissue tropism: the directed preferential affinity or targeting of gut-derived molecular signals (TMAO, LPS/endotoxin fragments, inflammatory mediators, gasotransmitters, etc.) toward specific remote tissues — in this case the heart and lungs — via circulatory and immune pathways. It is not a geometric or mechanical construct of “tangent axes.”
This aligns with the established biomedical usage of multi-organ axes (gut–heart axis, gut–lung axis, gut–lung–heart axis), which describe directed cross-organ signaling and tissue-specific effects rather than any mathematical tangent geometry.
